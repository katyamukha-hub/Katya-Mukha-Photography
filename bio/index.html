Bio/CV

Katya Mukha (1990) is an Independent documentary photographer. She was born in Rostov the Great - small town in central Russia, currently lives and works in Moscow.

She has engernearing background. But her interest on photography led her to Docdocdoc - School of modern photography in St. Petersburg - where she has been studied Documentary photography since 2019. Now she works on personal documentary projects. The focus of her work is modern social landscape and environment in Russia.
